◆ clip_space

ClipSpace fyuu_rhi::execution::Viewport::clip_space = ClipSpace::YUp

NDC Y orientation the application authors its clip space in.

Backends whose native convention differs (Vulkan is Y-down) flip the viewport to match. D3D12/WebGPU/OpenGL are natively Y-up, so both values coincide.
